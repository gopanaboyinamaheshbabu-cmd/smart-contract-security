## What pattern was added to my mental library?
- `storage` stores persistent blockchain state.
- `memory` is temporary and read-write during function execution.
- `calldata` is temporary and read-only, making it ideal for function parameters that don't need modification.
- Arrays are useful for storing ordered collections and iterating through data.
- Mappings store key-value pairs and provide constant-time (`O(1)`) lookups.
- Use mappings when fast retrieval by key is needed.
